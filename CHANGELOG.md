# Gamepad Reticle On Target

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Puts the controller's reticle just below your target's nameplate, so it stays on what you attack
- Vertical offset, in pixels
- Asks before turning on the nameplates it needs, and puts them back as they were when turned off
- Optional, all off by default, for friendly NPCs, neutral NPCs and friendly players' minions:
  - Hide their health bars, names or levels
  - World-style names for NPCs: colored, with their title, at the size you choose
- Settings in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Reticle On Target, or /grt
