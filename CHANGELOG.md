# Gamepad Reticle On Target

## 1.1.3

- The icon's orc vendor has its mohawk on its head, not floating above it

## 1.1.2

- The icon's orc vendor now wears a tunic with short sleeves, and its arms run off the bottom of the icon

## 1.1.1

- New icon: an orc vendor under a nameplate, with the reticle just above it

## 1.1.0

- The reticle now sits where the game shows its interaction icon on your target, just above the nameplate; Vertical offset counts from there
- On quest givers, it sits below the name instead, clear of the "!" or "?" the game draws above their head

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Puts the controller's reticle just below your target's nameplate, so it stays on what you attack
- Vertical offset, in pixels
- Asks before turning on the nameplates it needs, and puts them back as they were when turned off
- Optional, all off by default, for friendly NPCs, neutral NPCs and friendly players' minions:
  - Hide their health bars, names or levels
  - World-style names for NPCs: colored, with their title, at the size you choose
- Settings in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Reticle On Target, or /grt
